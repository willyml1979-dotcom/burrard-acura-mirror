Let’s take a look at some of the features offered in our new Acura models.

For a sharper personality, check out the brand-new Acura A-Spec lineups offered at Burrard Acura. A-Spec is more than just a model designation, it’s a statement that the owner makes on the road about avoiding compromise and making sure that every drive is an event to remember. A-Spec models are offered in a wide range of model lineups including the Acura TLX and Acura RDX compact SUV. Featuring a more aggressive body design, larger wheels, exclusive accents inside and out, and a sport-tuned suspension, Acura A-Spec is designed to give you the very best of everything in terms of design and performance. Make your vehicle shine and reflect your unique personality today with Acura A-Spec vehicles.

The first iteration of the Acura Super Handling all-wheel-drive system was introduced on the Acura RL all the way back in 2005. A full-time all-wheel-drive system, it combines a front-rear torque distribution network that can individually split the rear axle torque between the wheels to get the very best traction in any situation. With Acura SH-AWD, get some of the most efficient performance of any all-wheel-drive system in the world while also being completely undetectable in its functioning for the driver. The system figures out which wheels need the most torque and distributes the engine’s power accordingly.

AcuraWatch is a suite of active safety technologies that provides unparalleled safety in any situation. Features of the AcuraWatch system include adaptive cruise control, lane departure warning and prevention, forward collision warning, automatic braking, and a range of other technologies that aim to detect and warn the driver of any potential dangers. With AcuraWatch, you and your family are always safe on the road. The system keeps an eye out for potential dangers and protects your family without ever being too intrusive.

When it comes to dependability, we don’t usually associate luxury vehicles with reliability. That’s not the case with Acura whose reputation for never failing the driver is unmatched in Canada. With Acura, you never have a problem and your vehicle always works. With Acura, you get the latest technologies and a premium vehicle that’s known for lasting as long as you want without any of the quirks and gaps in reliability that come with many luxury vehicles out there. There’s a very impressive list of reasons to purchase a new Acura, but reliability and dependability are certainly at the top.
